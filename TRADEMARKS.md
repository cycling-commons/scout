# Scout name and logo

The Scout source code is [MIT licensed](LICENSE): use it, change it, ship your
own app with it.

The **Scout** name and the Scout logos are not covered by that licence. They
identify the official Scout apps by BikeCoders, and the copies in this repo are
here only so those apps can be built and released.

If you publish your own app or build based on this code, give it your own name
and icon. Files to replace include:

- `Hammerhead-Karoo/app/src/main/res/drawable/ic_scout_extension.xml` (launcher
  and extension icon)
- `Hammerhead-Karoo/docs/scout-karoo-icon.png` (update-manifest icon)
- the app names and icons in the other platform folders

Saying your app is "based on Scout" is fine; presenting it as Scout is not.
