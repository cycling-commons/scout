Scout for Karoo

User manual: install, set up, and tag your rides on a Hammerhead Karoo.

Scout lets you tag what you see on a ride (resupply stops, closures, road surface, hazards, scenery) with a tap on your Karoo. The tags, and readings from a rear radar if you have one, are saved inside your ride's normal .fit file. Scout has no account and uploads nothing.

1. What you need

  • A Hammerhead Karoo 2 or Karoo 3 with its latest software update installed. Scout is a Karoo extension, which needs recent Karoo software.
  • The Scout APK from the Scout releases page. Karoo builds are the releases named Scout for Karoo.
  • Optional: a rear radar (Garmin Varia or similar ANT+ radar).

2. Install Scout

Karoo 3: with the Hammerhead Companion app

  1. On your phone, open the releases page and long-press the scout-karoo-….apk link of the newest Scout for Karoo release, then Share it. The Companion app installs from a link, not from a downloaded file.
  2. Choose Hammerhead Companion as the share target.
  3. On the Karoo, accept the prompt and tap Install.

Hammerhead's guide: Companion App Sideloading.

Karoo 2 (or Karoo 3 over USB): with ADB on a computer

  1. Download the APK to your computer.
  2. Turn on USB debugging on the Karoo and connect it with a USB cable.
  3. Run adb install -r scout-karoo-<version>.apk in the folder with the APK.

Afterwards Scout appears in the Karoo app list. Opening it shows the ride state, an Open tagging button and a short setup reminder.

3. Set up the Scout page

You do this once per ride profile you want to tag with.

  1. On the Karoo, open the ride profile you'll use and add a data page.
  2. Pick the layout with a single field.
  3. Set that field to Scout (it's listed with the extension fields).
  4. Save the profile.
The Scout field must be on your ride profile. That is what switches Scout on for the ride. Without it Scout records nothing. You don't need to keep the page on screen: Scout keeps recording while you look at other pages.

Field size

Karoo lets you choose how much of a page each field gets. Scout adapts to the space you give it:

Scout's share of the pageWhat it shows
Whole page (recommended)The tag tiles. Tap them right on the page.
Half a page or moreThe same tiles, smaller. Fine for the six main tiles; the ten surface choices get tight.
Less than halfStatus only: ride state, tag count, radar. Tap it to open the full-screen tag screen.

Optional extras

  • Tag ride button. In your controller or button settings, assign the bonus action Tag ride to a button. It opens a full-screen copy of the tiles from any page. The Scout app's Open tagging button does the same.
  • Radar. Pair it as usual in Karoo Settings → Sensors. Scout has no pairing screen of its own.

4. Riding with Scout

Start, pause and end the ride on the Karoo as usual. Scout follows along by itself; there is no separate start button. Swipe to the Scout page whenever you want to tag something.

The basics

  • One tap tags. The tile lights up, the Karoo beeps, and the tag is written at the current second of your ride.
  • Choices. Most tiles open a set of follow-up choices. Tap one; it saves after a 3-second countdown. Tap a different choice during the countdown to change it. BACK returns to the main tiles without a tag.
  • No choice made? After 12 seconds the choices close. Closure, Notice, Surface and Scenery still save a tag without the detail; Resupply saves nothing.
  • Undo. After tapping OTHER, the tile counts down for 3 seconds; tap it again in that time to undo the tag. You hear a different, two-tone beep. For tiles with choices, fix a wrong pick during its countdown instead.
  • Counts. The number on a tile is how often you've used it this ride; the top right shows the ride's total.
  • Only while recording. Before the ride starts or while it's paused, the top right says so and taps do nothing.

Surface stretches

A surface tag starts a stretch: from here on the road is, say, gravel. While a stretch is open, the SURFACE tile stays lit with the surface name and a red Surface open: … END strip shows at the bottom. A stretch stays open until you end it: where the surface stops, tap the red strip, or tap SURFACE and then END. Picking a different surface instead closes the old stretch and starts a new one.

6. Radar

With a paired radar, the bottom strip of the Scout page shows how many cars have passed you this ride and an estimate of the last car's speed. Without one it reads no radar, or Connecting… while the Karoo looks for the radar. Radar readings are saved with your ride every second, whether or not you tag anything.

Karoo doesn't share the radar's own car speeds with apps, so the speed on screen is Scout's estimate from how quickly the gap closes, and the speed in the file is left empty.

7. Your data

Everything Scout records goes into the ride's normal .fit file, so it travels wherever your rides already go: the Hammerhead dashboard, Strava, or a USB copy. Each recorded second gets five extra values:

FieldMeaning
poi_typeWhat you tagged that second (0 = nothing)
poi_detailThe choice you picked, e.g. which surface
radar_countCars the radar sees behind you
radar_nearDistance to the nearest car
radar_speedAlways 255 (unknown) on Karoo

To look at them, open fit-viewer.html (in the release download) in a browser on your computer and load a ride file.

8. Troubleshooting

What you seeWhat to do
"Start a ride on Karoo before tagging" while ridingCheck the ride is actually recording, not paused or not yet started.
Scout page shows status text instead of tilesThe field is less than half a page. Give it a page of its own (see Set up).
Tapping a tile does nothingMake sure the ride is recording. If it still doesn't respond, use the Tag ride button or the Scout app's Open tagging screen, and let us know your Karoo model and OS version.
No beep on a tagCheck the Karoo's sound settings. The tile lighting up still confirms the tag.
Radar stays on no radarPair the radar in Karoo Settings → Sensors and check it's switched on and connected.
No Scout values in the ride fileThe Scout field wasn't on the ride profile you used, or the ride never started recording.

Report problems on the Scout issue tracker. Your Karoo model, OS version, a photo of the screen and the ride's .fit file help most.

9. Update or remove

  • Update: install the newer APK the same way you installed Scout. Your profile pages stay as they are.
  • Remove: uninstall Scout from the Karoo's app settings, then delete the Scout page from your ride profiles.
