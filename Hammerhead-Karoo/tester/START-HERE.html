Scout for Karoo: beta test

Version @VERSION@ · thank you for testing!

Scout lets you tag things you see on a ride (potholes, closures, road surface, resupply, scenery) with a tap on your Karoo. The tags, and readings from a rear radar if you have one, are saved inside your ride's normal .fit file. Scout has no account and doesn't upload anything.

This is the first test on a real Karoo. Things may break, and that's exactly what we want to find out. A crash or something odd is a useful result. Just note what you were doing when it happened.

1. Before you start

  • A Hammerhead Karoo 2 or Karoo 3, updated to Karoo OS 1.538.2049 or newer.
  • Optional: a rear radar (Garmin Varia or similar ANT+ radar). Scout works without one, but radar data is half of what we're testing.

2. Install Scout

Karoo 3: with the Hammerhead Companion app on your phone

  1. On your phone, open this download link: Scout @VERSION@ APK. Use the link, not the file inside the zip: the Companion app installs from a link. To type or paste it instead:
    @APK_URL@
  2. Use your phone's Share button and choose Hammerhead Companion.
  3. On the Karoo, accept the prompt and tap Install.

Hammerhead's guide: Companion App Sideloading.

Karoo 2 (or Karoo 3 via USB): with ADB on a computer

  1. Turn on USB debugging on the Karoo and connect it to your computer.
  2. Run adb install -r scout-karoo-@VERSION@.apk from the folder with this package.

Afterwards, Scout appears in the Karoo app list.

3. One-time setup on the Karoo

  1. Add the Scout field to a ride profile (required). Edit a data page in the profile you'll ride with and add the Scout field. Give it a decent-sized slot: it's a graphical field. Without this field, Scout doesn't record anything. You don't have to look at that page while riding; Scout records in the background on regardless of which page you view.
  2. Assign the Tag ride button (recommended). In your controller / button settings, assign the bonus action Tag ride to a button. Pressing it opens the tagging screen. You can also open it from the Scout app → Open tagging.
  3. Pair your radar (optional). Pair it as usual in Karoo Settings → Sensors. Scout has no pairing screen of its own.

4. Test rides (about 15 minutes each)

You only start and stop the ride on the Karoo, as usual. Scout follows along by itself.

Ride A: recording without tagging

  1. Start a ride. For the first few minutes, stay on a page without the Scout field.
  2. If you have a radar, ride somewhere cars pass you.
  3. Swipe to the Scout page and check that it shows recording, a tag count, and a radar line (e.g. Radar or 3 cars).
  4. End and save the ride.

Ride B: tagging

  1. Start a ride, then press your Tag ride button.
  2. Tap a few tiles. You should feel a buzz or hear a beep on every tag.
  3. Try a tile that opens a sub-menu (e.g. Danger), pick an option, then close the screen straight away. The tag should still be saved.
  4. Undo: tap the same tile again quickly; you get a different buzz.
  5. Surface: start a surface stretch and later end it with the End banner.
  6. Go back to your normal pages, ride a bit, end the ride.

Ride C: radar car count (if you have a radar)

  1. Ride a road with some traffic and roughly count the cars that overtake you.
  2. At the end, compare your count with the cars number on the Scout field.

5. What to send back

  • Karoo model (2 or 3) and Karoo OS version (Settings → About).
  • For each ride: radar yes/no, roughly how many tags you made, and, for Ride C, your car count and Scout's.
  • The ride .fit files. Get them from the Hammerhead dashboard (export activity), a service you sync to, or over USB.
  • Anything odd: crashes, a Scout field that stays on idle while riding, missing buzzes, a frozen screen. Photos of the Karoo screen help a lot.

6. Optional: look at your data

Open fit-viewer.html in a browser on your computer and load a ride .fit file. While you were recording, each second should have the Scout columns poi_type, poi_detail, radar_count, radar_near and radar_speed. Seconds where you tagged something show non-zero poi_type.

Known limitations in this beta

  • radar_speed in the file is always 255 (meaning "unknown"): Karoo doesn't give apps the radar's car speeds. The car speed on screen is an estimate.
  • Tagging only works while the ride is recording, not while paused.
  • Tags can't be made from the small Scout field; use the Tag ride screen.
